# Cold Outbound Email Writing & Review SOP

Consolidated from Manoj Agarwal + Rishabh Mansur review comments. Priority: Manoj wins on conflicts with Rishabh.

This is the canonical rule set for cold outbound email copy. `docs/content-agent-rules.md` and each kit's `CLAUDE.md` implement it, do not duplicate it in prose, edit this file and let the others reference it.

## 1. Email 1 (First Touch) Rules

- Never sell in email 1. No stats, no ROI numbers, no feature pitch, no product deep-dive.
- Email 1 is always positioned as an introduction, nothing more.
- Don't frame the opener around a narrow vertical (e.g. "manufacturing") unless the whole campaign is vertical-specific. Keep framing relevant but not overly narrow.
- Lead with a concrete, low-effort ask in sentence 1, e.g. same location/domain, offer to show how a similar problem was solved. Don't ask for the prospect's time or effort before giving them a reason.
- Don't lean only on the Xoxoday name for credibility. Position it as a global company, and customize the reference point to the specific product being pitched.
- Cut any recognition/R&R claim that could imply the prospect's current efforts go unrecognized.
- Never write short, thin emails. Email 1 should still be a complete, substantive introduction (relevance + context), just without the sell.

## 2. Sequencing & Cadence Rules

- Every email should read as if it's the first email. No "as discussed last time," "following up on my note last week," "third note," or any reference to sequence position.
- Never imply the sequence is ending. Avoid "before I close," "last mail," "closing the loop." The cadence is open-ended and continues until the prospect responds.
- Don't let the tone drift into frustration by the later emails. Stay even and professional throughout.
- The funnel should run Email -> WhatsApp -> SDR call in a waterfall, for every campaign, not email alone.
- Same buyer should never get repeated content. Build unique use-case emails per buyer, sequenced by role/industry.

## 3. Standard 7-Email Arc (Content Map)

Recommended structure per Manoj, repeat email 6's format from email 7 onward if the sequence continues:

1. Introduction only (no selling).
2. Why Empuls (stat/ROI paragraph moves here from email 1).
3. Empuls features, presented as bullets, not prose.
4. Case studies and customer testimonials.
5. G2 / Gartner / Forrester / Capterra mentions.
6. Dense recap: features, why Empuls, ratings, and customer names, packed concisely.
7. Onward: repeat email 6's structure/content.

## 4. Content Requirements

- Mention the Empuls AI agent in at least 2 emails across the sequence, 1-2 lines each, a plant, not a full pitch.
- Include named customers in at least some emails, matched to the prospect's industry.
- Include G2 / Gartner / Forrester / Capterra references at least once in the sequence.
- Always use "Xoxoday" as the company name, not sub-brand names like "Plum." Reference G2 and other proof points, don't leave them out.
- Cover why Xoxoday/Empuls (per website/PPT), ratings, customers, features, and security/compliance/sandbox across the arc, not all in one email.
- For CHRO personas specifically: cover rewards, benefits, perks, surveys, and social intranet, plus why Xoxoday, ratings, customers, features, security.
- Industry pain-point opener format: "In [Industry], some of the problems are A, B, D when it comes to employees, are you facing similar issues or anything different I should know about?" Then transition into how Xoxoday solves it.

## 5. Formatting Rules

- Use bullets for any list of 3+ items: modules, features, pain points. Never bury a list in a run-on sentence.
- Don't be stingy on word count. A complete email beats a short one, but keep it structured (bullets, not dense paragraphs).
- Avoid vague transition lines (e.g. "beyond the introduction"). Either say something concrete or cut it.
- Flag and rewrite any sentence whose meaning is ambiguous before it goes to review.
- Run a basic grammar and sentence-correction pass before submission. This should be caught before the reviewer sees it.
- Check for word-breaking/rendering issues (words splitting oddly across lines) before sending for review.
- Check for capitalization in between sentences. It should be formal and follow all rules of capitalization and lower case.

## 8. Pre-Send QA Checklist

- Email 1 read in isolation, does it read as a pitch? If yes, strip it back.
- No sequence-position language anywhere ("last," "third," "before I close").
- All lists of 3+ items are bulleted.
- Empuls AI agent mentioned in >= 2 emails, 1-2 lines each.
- At least one customer name included, matched to industry.
- At least one G2/Gartner/Forrester/Capterra reference included.
- Grammar and sentence-correction pass complete.
- No word-breaking/rendering issues on render check.
- Xoxoday used as company name (not sub-brand names).
- Funnel includes email + WhatsApp + SDR call waterfall.
- DND/unsubscribe exclusions applied before send.

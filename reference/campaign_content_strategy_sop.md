# Campaign Content & Messaging Strategy SOP

Canonical rule set for how campaigns are planned and messaged across email and LinkedIn. Complements [`cold-outbound-email-sop.md`](cold-outbound-email-sop.md) (Manoj Agarwal's cold outbound rules, which stay authoritative for email 1/sequencing specifics) - this doc covers content sourcing, persona/use-case planning, campaign types, and the pre-launch checklist.

## 1. Communication Style

- All emails and LinkedIn messages are professional, formal, and enterprise-focused.
- Messaging sounds natural and human, not automated or mechanical.
- No one-line emails. Every message provides enough context, relevance, and value to stand on its own.
- Avoid phrases that reference the sequence itself: "As mentioned in my last email," "This is my final follow-up," "This is my closing note" (this extends the sequence-position ban in `cold-outbound-email-sop.md` section 2 with these specific phrasings).

## 2. Email and LinkedIn Messaging

- The same core message (theme, proof stack, use case) carries across email and LinkedIn. Separate messaging strategies per channel are not required.
- LinkedIn can run shorter when the platform calls for it, but it is not a word-for-word copy of the email - same substance, native format per channel.
- One common message per persona/use-case reduces confusion and keeps email and LinkedIn consistent with each other.

## 3. Approved Content Sources

Campaign messaging draws only from approved Xoxoday material:
- Xoxoday website
- Product decks
- Help documentation
- Internal wiki
- Approved case studies and customer references

Do not introduce unsupported claims or unverified product information. If a fact isn't in one of these sources, don't use it, ask instead of inventing it.

## 4. Persona and Use-Case Planning

- Do not build a separate email sequence per use case for the same persona.
- Consolidate the relevant use cases into one thoughtfully structured sequence.
- The sequence gradually introduces different problems, use cases, capabilities, and proof points as it progresses, it doesn't front-load everything into one email.
- Avoid sending too many overlapping emails to the same person (same persona should not be targeted by multiple concurrent campaigns, see section 7).

## 5. Campaign Content Coverage

A complete sequence covers a balanced mix of, distributed logically across emails rather than crammed into one:
- Xoxoday's credibility and trust
- Recognized global customers (anonymized per the naming rule in `cold-outbound-email-sop.md`)
- Relevant case studies
- Customer testimonials
- Key features related to the use case
- Business outcomes and value proposition
- Differentiation from competitors
- G2 ratings and third-party validation
- Global reach, enterprise readiness, and support capabilities

Not every email needs to cover everything.

## 6. Campaign Types

**Use-Case Campaigns**
- Built around defined personas, industries, and use cases.
- Planned and executed as repeatable campaigns.
- Reviewed approximately once every six months.
- Updated when products, messaging, proof points, or market conditions change.

**Dynamic Campaigns**
Built around time-sensitive signals:
- Job changes
- Leadership appointments
- Intent signals
- Funding announcements
- Expansion into new markets
- Technology changes
- Hiring activity
- Relevant company news

Planned separately from Use-Case Campaigns since they require current context and timely execution (this is the natural home for the Bright Data LinkedIn-signal pipeline's `personalized_line` output).

## 7. Final Quality Check

Before launching any campaign, confirm:
- The target persona is clearly defined.
- Multiple use cases are consolidated where appropriate.
- The message is professional and human.
- Claims are supported by approved Xoxoday sources.
- The sequence includes sufficient context and proof.
- The same persona is not being targeted through overlapping campaigns.
- Email and LinkedIn communication remain consistent.

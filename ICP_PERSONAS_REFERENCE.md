## HOW TO USE THIS DOCUMENT

**When drafting copy:**

1. **Identify the target persona** — Are you selling to a CHRO, VP HR, or HR Ops lead?
2. **Identify the use case** — Employee Recognition, Commission Automation, Loyalty, etc.?
3. **Cross-reference the table** — See which product fits, what proof style lands, what pain point to open with.
4. **Read the HUMANVOICE_COPY_GUIDE.md** — Apply the tone rules + this persona's specific pain point.
5. **Draft the sequence** — Use the email copy frameworks + this ICP context for personalization.

**Example:**

- **Persona:** VP HR at 2,000-person healthcare company
- **Use Case:** Employee Recognition (Empuls angle)
- **Geography:** USA
- **Proof Style (from PERSONA-TO-COPY FRAMEWORK MAPPING):** Timeline + adoption jump
- **Pain Point:** Manual recognition scattered across Slack, spreadsheets, annual awards
- **Opening:** "Most People teams at {{company_name}}'s size run recognition on a patchwork — Slack shoutouts, spreadsheets for anniversaries, an annual awards night. [Company Name] fixed that by centralizing it in one platform..."
- **Proof Point:** "A 3,500-person healthcare system rolled it out in 60 days across all locations. R&R live for frontline and office staff in the same system."
- **CTA:** "Worth 15 minutes to walk through how they structured it?"

## CROSS-SELL SIGNALS

Watch for these buying signals in your ICP data to suggest multiple Xoxoday products:

| Signal | Suggest |
|---|---|
| They have *employee* recognition program talk | Add Plum for employee perks/gifting; Compass if they're also a sales organization |
| They mention *referral program* | Plum (rewards API) is the obvious fit; if tech-forward, Plum + Empuls for employee incentive alignment |

| They have *high frontline/field teams* | Empuls (recognition at scale) + Plum (global rewards) combo is strong |
| They're *highly regulated* (healthcare, insurance, financial) | Lead with Compass (compliance + audit trail); Empuls second (training rewards) |

## PERSONA DEPTH: CHAMPION VS. ECONOMIC BUYER VS. INFLUENCER

**Your copy strategy changes by who you're reaching:**

| Persona Type | Copy Strategy | How to Reach | Proof Style |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Economic Buyer** (CHRO, VP, C-suite) | Prove business case, board-level impact, strategic alignment | Email (formal introduction + business case) | G2 rating, enterprise case study, cost/headcount recovery |
| **Champion** (Director, manager) | Prove day-to-day adoption, tool consolidation, their credibility upward | Email + LinkedIn (peer-to-peer tone) | Timeline, user adoption %, internal proof |
| **Influencer/User** (HRBP, HR admin, ops lead) | Prove friction reduction, their job gets easier, no extra work | Slack or direct message (casual, helpful) | Speed stats, one-click proof, before/after workflow |

## ICP SCORING AT A GLANCE

When you have a list of prospects, score them on these dimensions (from the ICP):

| Dimension | High-Value Signal | Low-Value Signal |
|---|---|---|
| **Company Size** | 500-5,000 FTE (Empuls); 200+ FTE (Compass); 100+ FTE (Loyalife) | < 100 FTE or > 50,000 (enterprise with internal solutions) |
| **Use Case Fit** | Explicit pain (e.g., "commission disputes," "employee gifting") | Generic hiring or no current program |
| **Geography** | USA, India, Europe (strong Xoxoday footprint) | Emerging markets, single-country-only |
| **Buyer Title** | CHRO, VP HR, VP Sales, VP Loyalty, Head of Ops | Coordinator, associate, junior title (long sales cycle) |
| **Maturity** | They have a current program (even if broken) | No program at all (adoption/category education needed) |

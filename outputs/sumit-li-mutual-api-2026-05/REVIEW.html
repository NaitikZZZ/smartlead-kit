5. Format rules followed

These rules come from Xoxoday's outbound playbook and are not optional.

  • Subject line under 5 words.
  • Email body: 4 sentences max, each under 15 words, each on its own line.
  • No em-dashes or en-dashes anywhere.
  • No "Best, Avipsa" sign-offs in the body. Smartlead auto-appends signature at the account level.
  • No "Hope you're doing well" or similar filler.
  • First touch carries an insight or a question, not a product pitch.
  • Numbers in the copy are real and traceable to xoxoday.com.

If a reviewer suggests a change, please keep these rules intact.

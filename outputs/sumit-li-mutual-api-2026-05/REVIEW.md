## 5. Format rules followed

These rules come from Xoxoday's outbound playbook and are not optional.

1. Subject line under 5 words.
2. Email body: 4 sentences max, each under 15 words, each on its own line.
3. No em-dashes or en-dashes anywhere.
4. No "Best, Avipsa" sign-offs in the body. Smartlead auto-appends signature at the account level.
5. No "Hope you're doing well" or similar filler.
6. First touch carries an insight or a question, not a product pitch.
7. Numbers in the copy are real and traceable to xoxoday.com.

If a reviewer suggests a change, please keep these rules intact.

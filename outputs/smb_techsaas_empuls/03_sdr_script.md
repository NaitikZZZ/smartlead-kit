# Empuls SDR Calling Script

> Buyer: Head / Director / HRBP / VP HR at Indian SMB Tech SaaS
> Use after Email 2 and LinkedIn connect. Day 5 = first dial, Day 8 = second dial + voicemail.
> Goal of every call: book a 20 min discovery, not pitch.

---

## D5, Call 1

### Opener (15 seconds, no fluff)

> Hi {{first_name}}, this is [SDR name] from Xoxoday. I know I am calling cold, can I borrow 30 seconds to tell you why and you can decide if it is worth a real conversation?

[Pause for yes / sigh / no]

### If "yes" or silence

> Most People leaders we work with at SaaS firms your size are running R&R across 4 places: Slack shoutouts, ad-hoc gift cards, Excel for anniversaries, and an annual awards night. We built Empuls to pull all of that into one platform that lives inside Slack or Teams. Brenntag, Bosch, and Adobe are on it.
>
> Two questions before I let you go: 1) Is recognition on a tool or stitched together right now? 2) Is engagement or attrition something the team is solving for in 2026?

### If "we have a tool" (Vantage Circle, Workhuman, Reward Gateway, Plumm)

> Got it. Most teams that switch to us from those tools either want India + global rewards in one place, deeper Slack and Teams integration, or surveys built in instead of a separate Culture Amp. If any of those are open, worth 20 mins. If everything is humming, I will get out of your way.

### If "send me an email"

> Done, I will keep it tight. Quick check first so the email is actually useful: how many FTE are you running R&R for, and is the bigger gap recognition itself or the rewards catalogue?

### If "not now"

> Fair. Quick check, is "not now" 1 quarter, 2 quarters, or 2027? I will note it and only come back when it makes sense.

### Booking the meeting

> Great, 20 mins. I will send a Calendly with three slots this week. If none work, just reply with two times. Confirming your email is {{email}}, correct?

## D8, Call 2 + Voicemail

### Voicemail (30 seconds)

> Hi {{first_name}}, [SDR name] from Xoxoday again. Sent you an email last week about Empuls, our employee engagement and rewards platform. We help SaaS HR teams in your headcount band consolidate R&R, automate milestones, and unlock a global rewards catalogue. If engagement is on your radar this year, I would love 20 minutes. Final email coming, then I will back off. All the best.

### If they pick up on the second attempt

> Hi {{first_name}}, [SDR name] from Xoxoday. I will not take more than a minute. I called and emailed earlier about Empuls. The reason I called twice is that I genuinely think the platform fits {{company_name}}'s setup. Two paths: I can send a 90-second loom that walks through it, or we book 20 mins later this week. Which is easier?

## Objection handlers (cheat sheet)

| Objection | One-liner response |
|---|---|
| "We use Slack shoutouts, it works." | Most teams say that until eNPS dips. Curious, do you have a single dashboard that shows who is recognized this month and who is not? |
| "Vantage Circle / Workhuman is fine." | Both are good. Where we win in India is the breadth of the catalogue (gold, local brands, experiences) plus surveys built in. Worth 20 mins to compare. |
| "Send a deck." | I can do better. Can I send a 90-second loom of how recognition flows inside Slack? |
| "Budget is locked." | Understood. Most pilots are paid out of the existing R&R budget consolidated into one platform, not new spend. Worth 15 mins to map that? |
| "Talk to my CHRO." | Happy to. Can you intro me, or is it better if I drop your name? |
| "We are too small." | We have customers from 150 FTE. Often the smaller you are, the more leverage one platform gives you. |

## Disposition codes (for the SDR to log)

- `MEETING_BOOKED`
- `INTERESTED_NURTURE` (re-engage in 30 days)
- `WRONG_PERSON` (route to CHRO / Head HR contact)
- `NOT_NOW_Q3` / `NOT_NOW_Q4` / `NOT_NOW_2027`
- `COMPETITOR_INSTALLED` (capture vendor + renewal date)
- `DNC` (mark in HubSpot, suppress across channels)

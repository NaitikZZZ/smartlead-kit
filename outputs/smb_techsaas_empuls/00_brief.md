## Why Empuls (the angle)

HR leaders at Indian Tech SaaS companies in the 200 to 5,000 FTE band feel a specific stack of pain:

- Recognition is fragmented across Slack shoutouts, ad-hoc gift cards, and one annual awards night
- Service anniversaries and birthdays are tracked in Excel by HRBPs, often missed
- Hybrid + remote tech teams are the hardest to engage, regardless of where they sit
- eNPS surveys take quarters to run, never tied to action
- Reward catalogues are India-only or US-only, not both
- Attrition in Indian SaaS hovers at 18 to 25 percent annually, with R&R cited as a top 3 lever

Empuls puts all of it in one platform: peer recognition (Slack/Teams native), automated milestones, surveys + lifecycle pulses, perks (zero-cost discounts), and a 10M+ rewards catalogue across 175+ countries with a strong India catalogue. Verified Indian tech/IT customers (per public case studies): KPIT Technologies, Prodevans Technologies, Bahwan CyberTek. Other verified customers across industries: Brenntag, ANCA, SPI Cinemas, FCA India.

## Why Compass (the angle)

Mid-market Indian SaaS sales orgs run their incentives, AE commissions AND partner SPIFFs AND contest payouts AND lead-reg bonuses, on Excel + Google Sheets. Symptoms the buyer feels every month:

- 5 to 7 days of RevOps + Finance time burned reconciling commissions and partner payouts
- Reps and partners don't trust the math, escalate disputes, lose trust in plan changes
- No real-time visibility into quota attainment, accelerators, SPIFF earnings, or partner tier
- Plan changes (new tiers, contests, kickers) take weeks to roll out for both internal and channel
- CFO can't predict variable comp at quarter-end across reps + partners
- Partner payouts go out as INR bank transfers, no flexibility for international partners

Compass solves this end-to-end across both motions: plan modeling, real-time dashboards for reps and partners, gamified leaderboards, automated payout via Plum (100+ countries, gift cards/cash/experiences), and full audit trail.

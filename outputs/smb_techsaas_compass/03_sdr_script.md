# Compass SDR Calling Script

> Buyer: VP / Head / Director of Sales at Indian SMB Tech SaaS
> Use after Email 2 and LinkedIn connect. Day 5 = first dial, Day 8 = second dial + voicemail.
> Goal of every call: book a 20 min discovery, not pitch.

---

## D5, Call 1

### Opener (15 seconds, no fluff)

> Hi {{first_name}}, this is [SDR name] from Xoxoday. I know I am calling cold, can I borrow 30 seconds to tell you why and you can decide if it is worth a real conversation?

[Pause for yes / sigh / no]

### If "yes" or silence (DIRECT SALES SEGMENT)

> Most VP Sales we work with at SaaS companies your size are still running commissions on Sheets. RevOps bleeds 5 to 7 days a month, reps shadow-track their numbers, and rolling out a SPIFF takes a week. We built Compass, our sales incentives module inside Empuls, to fix that end to end. Pepsico, Capgemini, and Aditya Birla Capital are on it.
>
> Two questions before I let you go: 1) Is comp run on Sheets or a tool today? 2) Is fixing this on the priority list for 2026?

### If "yes" or silence (CHANNEL SALES SEGMENT, 4 leads: Atlys, Spectranet, Emudhra)

> Most channel sales leaders we work with at SaaS firms your size are running partner SPIFFs and commission overrides on a Sheet shared with 50 to 200 partners. Partners cannot see their own attainment, escalations pile up on channel managers, and contest payouts take weeks. Compass, the sales incentives module inside Empuls, runs the plan logic. Payouts flow through Plum so partners in India and abroad can take it as gift cards, cash, or experiences in their own country.
>
> Two questions: 1) How many active partners is {{company_name}} running incentives for today? 2) Where does most of the friction sit, plan rollout, payout, or visibility for partners?

### If "we have a tool" (Spiff, Everstage, Xactly, in-house)

> Got it. Most teams that switch to us from those tools either want India-specific support, faster plan rollouts, or the integrated rewards engine on payouts. If any of those are open issues, worth 20 mins. If everything is humming, I will get out of your way.

### If "send me an email"

> Done, I will keep it tight. One question first so the email is actually useful: how many AEs are you running comp for right now, and is comp the bottleneck or is plan design the bottleneck?

### If "not now"

> Fair. Quick check, is "not now" 1 quarter, 2 quarters, or 2026? I will note it and only come back when it makes sense.

### Booking the meeting

> Great, 20 mins. I will send a Calendly with three slots this week. If none work, just reply with two times. Confirming your email is {{email}}, correct?

## D8, Call 2 + Voicemail

### Voicemail (30 seconds)

> Hi {{first_name}}, [SDR name] from Xoxoday again. Sent you an email last week about Compass, our sales commission platform. We help SaaS sales orgs in your headcount band move comp off Sheets, cut disputes by 80 percent, and roll out SPIFFs in days, not weeks. If sales comp is on your radar this quarter, I would love 20 minutes. I will drop a final email and then back off. All the best.

### If they pick up on the second attempt

> Hi {{first_name}}, [SDR name] from Xoxoday. I will not take more than a minute. I called and emailed earlier about Compass. The reason I called twice is that I genuinely think the platform fits {{company_name}}'s motion. Two paths: I can send a 90-second loom that walks through it, or we book 20 mins later this week. Which is easier?

## Objection handlers (cheat sheet)

| Objection | One-liner response |
|---|---|
| "We use Excel and it is fine." | Most teams say that until reps stop trusting it. Curious, do reps see their attainment in real time today? |
| "Spiff/Everstage already pitched us." | Both are good products. Where we win is India support, faster plan changes, and the built-in rewards engine on payouts. Worth 20 mins to compare. |
| "Compass is just for internal reps, our pain is partners." | Same module runs both. Plan logic for overrides, SPIFFs, lead-reg, tier accelerators applies to partners exactly the same way. Plum handles partner payouts in 100+ countries. Worth 20 mins to map your partner motion. |
| "We are looking at a partner-loyalty platform like Loyalife." | Different problem. Loyalife is for long-term, points-based partner programs. If you want quick-cycle SPIFFs and quarterly contests, Compass is the right module. We can do both inside one Xoxoday account. |
| "Send a deck." | I can do better, can I send a 90-second loom of the dashboard? Decks are dead. |
| "Budget is locked." | Understood. Most pilots we run are paid out of the RevOps efficiency line, not new budget. Worth 15 mins to map that? |
| "Talk to RevOps, not me." | Happy to. Can you intro me, or is it better if I drop your name? |

## Disposition codes (for the SDR to log)

- `MEETING_BOOKED`
- `INTERESTED_NURTURE` (re-engage in 30 days)
- `WRONG_PERSON` (route to RevOps / Sales Ops contact)
- `NOT_NOW_Q3` / `NOT_NOW_Q4` / `NOT_NOW_2027`
- `COMPETITOR_INSTALLED` (capture vendor + renewal date)
- `DNC` (mark in HubSpot, suppress across channels)

**How we picked them:**
- Hiring growth >20% (company is adding headcount fast)
- Employee size >200 (has a real R&R and incentive budget)
- ABM tier 0.1 (top priority accounts)

## 2. Call opening — base script

Keep it under 20 seconds. Do not read verbatim, adapt to the specific email the lead received (see per-lead talk track).

**Opener (adapt the hook to the email you sent):**

> "Hi [First name], this is [SDR name] from Xoxoday. I sent you a note earlier this week about [specific hook from Email 1 — e.g. 'how [Company] is scaling its sales team through the 70% ARR growth' or 'keeping engagement up while [Company] hires across Austin and Bangalore']. Did I catch you at an okay moment for 30 seconds?"

If yes, continue. If no, ask for a better time and end the call. Do not push.

**One-line reason for the call:**

> "The reason I'm reaching out specifically is that companies growing headcount as fast as [Company] tend to hit a wall where their recognition and rewards programs stop scaling with the business. I wanted to see whether that is showing up on your side yet, or whether you've already got it locked down."

**Qualifier (pick one, matched to the email angle):**

- Growth / hiring angle: *"As you are hiring fast into [region or function], how are you keeping engagement and retention holding up for the new joiners in the first 90 days?"*
- Sales team angle: *"Your sales org is growing quickly. How are you handling incentive and SPIFF delivery for reps across regions today, and who owns that internally — HR or Sales Ops?"*
- Global angle: *"With employees in [X countries], how are you handling rewards fulfillment and tax compliance across geographies today?"*
- Recognition angle: *"What does peer-to-peer recognition look like for you today, and is it driving the behavior you want?"*

## 3. Discovery questions — use 2 or 3 max

- "Who owns rewards and recognition today? Is it centralized in HR, or does each business unit run its own?"
- "What tools are you using right now? Is it one platform, or a stack of gift card portals, survey tools, and internal shoutouts?"
- "How are you measuring whether the program is actually moving engagement or retention?"
- "What is your budget cycle for HR tech? Is rewards and recognition a line item or part of a broader engagement budget?"
- "If I could show you how [similar company] consolidated five tools into one and cut fulfillment cost by 20 to 30%, would that be worth a 20-minute call with our team?"

## 4. Objection handling

**"We already have a platform / we use Bonusly / Achievers / Workhuman."**
> "Totally fair. Most of our customers came from one of those. The two things we hear after they switched are global fulfillment across 100+ countries without the reps taking a cut, and consolidating sales incentives and employee recognition into one admin stack. Worth a 20-minute compare?"

**"Not a priority right now."**
> "Understood. Can I ask what is higher on the list for this quarter? If it is retention or hiring velocity, there is usually a rewards angle we can tie into it. If not, I will stop bothering you."

**"Send me an email."**
> "Happy to. So I send you the right thing, are you more focused on employee recognition, sales incentives, or global rewards fulfillment right now?"

**"We do not have budget."**
> "Got it. Is there a budget holder above you on this, or is this something that gets revisited at your next planning cycle? I will just time my follow-up to that, no pressure now."

**"Who gave you my number?"**
> "Public sources, specifically [LinkedIn / company site]. Happy to take you off the list if you prefer, no issue at all."

## 5. Close and next step

Goal of the call is one of three outcomes, in this order of preference:

1. **Booked meeting** with an AE: 20 to 30 minutes, this week or next. Confirm calendar invite on the call.
2. **Warm referral** to the right buyer internally if this lead is not the owner.
3. **Explicit opt-out** so we stop spending cycles on this lead.

**Meeting ask:**
> "Based on what you just said, I think the 20 minutes with [AE name] would be worth it. I have Thursday at 10am or Friday at 2pm your time. Which works?"

**Referral ask:**
> "Who inside [Company] actually owns rewards and recognition today? If you can point me to them I will stop bugging you on this one."

## 6. Disposition codes to log after each call

Use these exactly, for downstream reporting:

- `MEETING_BOOKED` — AE meeting scheduled, invite sent
- `WARM_REFERRAL` — lead pointed you to the real owner, capture name and email
- `INTERESTED_NURTURE` — had the conversation, not ready now, follow up in 30 to 60 days
- `NOT_A_FIT` — wrong persona or company does not qualify
- `DO_NOT_CALL` — lead asked to be removed
- `NO_ANSWER` — no pickup, voicemail left or not
- `GATEKEEPER` — reached assistant or switchboard only
- `BAD_NUMBER` — phone disconnected or wrong person
